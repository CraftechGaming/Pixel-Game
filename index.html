Touch

Use ◀ ▶ to walk, ▲ to jump and ▼ to go down. The Dig / Build button chooses what happens when you tap the world. Hold to keep digging.

Grow anything

Plant seeds on grass, dirt or farmland. Plants grow faster near water, on farmland and in the rain. Use Fertilizer on a plant to grow it right away.

A House Seed grows a whole cottage. A Tower Seed grows a lookout tower. A Wonder Seed grows a surprise.

Water

Water flows downhill and spreads out. A Water Spring makes endless water and a Sponge soaks it up.

Parkour

Touch a green Start flag, reach the blue Checkpoints and finish at the checkered flag. Build your own course with Bounce Pads, Speed Pads and Crumble Blocks.

PixelRealms

Build homes, grow anything, and explore villages, a city and nine parkour courses.

The same seed always makes the same world. Share yours with friends.
