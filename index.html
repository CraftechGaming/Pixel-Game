Menu

Touch

Drag on the left to walk. Drag on the right to look. Tap to place, hold to mine, or use the pickaxe and block buttons.

Survival

Hold to mine and the block drops for you to collect. Start by mining a tree for logs. Open Inventory to turn logs into planks, sticks and a pickaxe. Stone needs a pickaxe, iron needs a stone pickaxe, gold and gems need iron.

Cook ore with coal to get ingots. Eat apples and bread to heal. Sleep in a bed to skip the night.

Grow things

Plant seeds, saplings and flowers on grass or dirt. Fertilizer grows a plant right away. A House Seed grows a whole cottage and a Wonder Seed grows a surprise.

Parkour

Race eleven rivals. Jump the sweepers, dodge the hammers and pushers, ride the moving platforms, and get to the finish arch. In the Knockout Cup only the fastest go through to the next round.
